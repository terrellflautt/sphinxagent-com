## Week 4: Tips — Chatbot Best Practices (Apr 24-30)

### Day 22 — Apr 24 (Thu)
**9 AM:** Chatbot tip 1: Start narrow. Don't try to make an agent that knows everything. Make one that's great at one thing. Then expand.

**1 PM:** Chatbot tip 2: Write your agent's responses like a human wrote them. Short sentences. No corporate jargon. If you wouldn't say it out loud, your agent shouldn't type it.

**6 PM:** Chatbot tip 3: Always give users an escape hatch. "Talk to a human" should be available at every point in the conversation. Trapping users in a bot loop is how you lose customers. sphinxagent.ai

### Day 23 — Apr 25 (Fri)
**9 AM:** Chatbot tip 4: Test with real questions, not the ones you hope people will ask. Look at your actual support tickets. Those are the questions your agent needs to answer.

**1 PM:** Chatbot tip 5: Your agent's first message matters more than anything else. Don't say "Hello! How can I help you today?" Say what the agent can actually do. "I can help with orders, returns, and account questions."

**6 PM:** Chatbot tip 6: Review your agent's conversations weekly. Find where it struggles. Improve those specific areas. Iteration beats perfection at launch. sphinxagent.ai

### Day 24 — Apr 26 (Sat)
**9 AM:** Chatbot tip 7: Set expectations. If your agent takes 5 seconds to look something up, tell the user. "Checking your order status now..." is better than silence.

**1 PM:** Chatbot tip 8: Don't fake personality. If your brand is straightforward, make your agent straightforward. Forced humor in a support bot is painful for everyone.

**6 PM:** Chatbot tip 9: Handle "I don't understand" gracefully. The agent should rephrase, ask a clarifying question, or offer to connect with a human. Never just repeat itself. sphinxagent.ai

### Day 25 — Apr 27 (Sun)
**9 AM:** The Sphinx's golden rule of conversation design: if a user has to repeat themselves, you've already failed.

**1 PM:** Chatbot tip 10: Keep conversations short. If your agent needs more than 5 exchanges to resolve something simple, the flow needs work.

**6 PM:** Chatbot tip 11: Use buttons and quick replies where possible. Typing is effort. Tapping is easy. Reduce friction at every step. sphinxagent.ai

### Day 26 — Apr 28 (Mon)
**9 AM:** Prompt engineering tip: Be specific about what your agent should NOT do. "Never discuss competitor products" is clearer than "stay on topic."

**1 PM:** Prompt engineering tip: Give your agent example conversations. Show it what a good interaction looks like. LLMs learn from examples better than from rules.

**6 PM:** Prompt engineering tip: Define your agent's knowledge boundaries explicitly. "If asked about X, say you don't have that information and suggest contacting support@company.com." sphinxagent.ai

### Day 27 — Apr 29 (Tue)
**9 AM:** Conversation design tip: Map out your top 10 user intents before building anything. If you don't know what users will ask, you're guessing. And guessing builds bad agents.

**1 PM:** Conversation design tip: Every dead end in your flow is a user who leaves frustrated. Audit your flows for dead ends. The Sphinx has zero tolerance for them.

**6 PM:** Conversation design tip: Your fallback response is the most important one. It fires when everything else fails. Make it helpful, not apologetic. sphinxagent.ai

### Day 28 — Apr 30 (Wed)
**9 AM:** Week 4 summary: the best AI agents aren't the smartest. They're the best designed. Conversation flow, clear boundaries, graceful failures — these matter more than model size.

**1 PM:** One more tip: launch before you're ready. A simple agent that handles 5 questions well is more useful than a complex one that handles 50 questions poorly. Ship, learn, iterate.

**6 PM:** Tips week is over. Next week we talk pricing — specifically, why enterprise chatbot platforms charge so much and what you actually get for the money. sphinxagent.ai
