Refer a Friend, Get Rewarded

Share Sphinx Agent with your network. When they subscribe, you both get free months on your plan.

Share Your Link

Copy your unique referral link from the dashboard and share it with friends, colleagues, or your audience.

Your friend signs up through your link and subscribes to any paid plan. They get a free trial as a welcome bonus.

3

You Both Save

Once they make a qualifying payment, you both receive credits applied automatically to your next billing cycle.

What You Both Get

Your Referral Subscribes To They Get You Get
Any Monthly Plan 1 month free trial 1 month credit*
Any Yearly Plan 2 months free trial 2 months credit*

*Credit is based on the referred customer's plan price, not yours. For example, if you're on the Enterprise plan and refer someone who subscribes to the Starter plan, you receive a credit equal to 1 month of the Starter plan ($19).

Referral Terms

  • ✓ Credits are applied automatically to your Stripe balance and deducted from your next invoice(s).
  • ✓ There is no limit to the number of people you can refer. Each qualifying referral earns additional credit.
  • ✓ If you're on the Free plan, credits are stored and applied when you subscribe to a paid plan.
  • ✓ Monthly referrals: Your credit is issued after your referral makes their 2nd monthly payment. This ensures they are a committed subscriber.
  • ✓ Yearly referrals: Your credit is issued after your referral makes their 1st payment, since yearly plans represent a committed subscription.
  • ✓ Credit amounts are based on the referred customer's plan price, not your own plan price.
  • ✓ Self-referrals are not permitted. Referral codes cannot be used on accounts associated with the same person.
  • ✓ Sphinx Agent reserves the right to modify or discontinue the referral program at any time with 30 days notice.
  • ✓ Credits are non-transferable and cannot be redeemed for cash.
