Do Not Sell My Personal Information

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Rights

Sphinx Agent does NOT sell your personal information to third parties. We use your data only to provide and improve our service.

What Data We Collect

When you use Sphinx Agent, we collect the following information:

  • Google Profile Information: Your name, email address, and profile picture when you sign in with Google OAuth.
  • Chat Messages: Conversations between your AI agents and end users, stored for 90 days to maintain chat context.
  • Usage Statistics: Aggregate counts of messages sent per month, number of agents created, and feature usage for billing and analytics.
  • Payment Information: Billing details processed securely through Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers on our servers.

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your personal information to any third party.
  • We do not trade your data with other companies for marketing purposes.
  • We do not rent access to your data or user lists.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or share data with ad networks.
  • We do not share your chat messages with anyone other than the AI model providers necessary to generate responses.

Your CCPA Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • Delete your personal information held by us.
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information (though we do not sell it).
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your CCPA rights.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of your rights under the CCPA, or if you have questions about our data practices, please contact us:

Email: Contact Us

Address: 9741 N Lake Creek Pkwy, STE C-219, Austin, TX 78717

We will respond to verifiable consumer requests within 45 days of receipt.

For more details about how we handle your data, please read our full Privacy Policy.
