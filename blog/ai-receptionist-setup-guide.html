AI Receptionist: How to Set Up an AI Phone Receptionist for Your Business

You are losing clients right now because nobody answered the phone. Here is how to fix that in under 30 minutes with an AI receptionist that works around the clock.

Terrell K. Flautt

Founder, SnapIT Software

Here is the math that should keep you up at night: 80% of callers who reach voicemail do not leave a message. They hang up and call the next business on the list. If you run a medical office, a law firm, a salon, a contracting company, or any service business where the phone matters — every missed call is lost revenue. Not potential revenue. Actual revenue that walked out the door because nobody picked up.

The same thing happens on your website. A visitor lands on your page at 9 PM, has a question about your services, sees no way to get an answer, and leaves. They do not come back.

An AI receptionist fixes both problems. It answers every call. It responds to every website visitor. It books appointments, answers questions about your services and hours, routes urgent matters to the right person, and takes messages when your team is unavailable. It does this 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for a fraction of what you would pay a part-time human receptionist.

This guide walks you through setting one up using Sphinx Agent. The whole process takes about 30 minutes.

What a Good AI Receptionist Actually Handles

Before we get into setup, here is what you should expect from an AI receptionist that is properly configured:

  • Greeting visitors and callers. A natural, on-brand welcome — not a robotic phone tree. "Thanks for calling Dr. Rivera's office, how can I help you?" Not "Press 1 for appointments, press 2 for billing."
  • Answering common questions. "What are your hours?" "Do you accept Blue Cross?" "How much does a consultation cost?" "Where are you located?" These questions make up 60-70% of inbound inquiries for most service businesses. Your AI handles all of them instantly.
  • Scheduling appointments. The AI checks your available time slots, confirms the service type, books the appointment, and sends a confirmation. No back-and-forth email chains. No hold music.
  • Routing to the right person. "I need to speak with someone about my case" goes to the attorney. "I have a billing question" goes to the office manager. "This is an emergency" gets escalated immediately.
  • Taking messages after hours. When nobody is available, the AI captures the caller's name, number, reason for calling, and urgency level. It sends that to your team via email or text so they can follow up first thing in the morning.
  • Qualifying leads. The AI can ask the right questions to determine if a prospect is a good fit before they ever reach your team. What service do they need? What is their timeline? Are they in your service area?

Step-by-Step Setup: Your AI Receptionist in 30 Minutes

Step 1: Create Your Account

Go to sphinxagent.ai and sign up. Free tier gets you started with web chat and 100 conversations per month. No credit card required.

Step 2: Create a New Agent

From your dashboard, click New Agent. You will see several agent types. Select Receptionist. This pre-loads the right conversation flows for greeting, routing, scheduling, and message-taking.

Step 3: Configure Your Business Details

Fill in the basics:

  • Business name — exactly as you want the AI to say it
  • Business hours — standard hours, lunch breaks, holiday schedules
  • Services offered — list every service with a brief description and price range if applicable
  • Staff directory — names, roles, and what types of inquiries each person handles
  • Location and contact info — address, phone, email, parking instructions

Step 4: Build Your Knowledge Base

This is where the AI gets smart. Add everything a receptionist would need to know:

  • Your FAQ — the 20 questions you get asked most often
  • Scheduling rules — appointment durations, buffer times, which staff handle which services
  • Insurance or payment info — what you accept, co-pay ranges, financing options
  • Policies — cancellation policy, no-show fees, what to bring to a first visit
  • After-hours protocol — what counts as urgent, who to escalate to, what can wait until morning

You can type this in manually, paste from an existing document, or upload a PDF. The AI processes it all.

Step 5: Connect Your Calendar

Link your Google Calendar, Outlook, or Calendly account. The AI will read your availability in real time and only offer time slots that are actually open. When it books an appointment, the event shows up on your calendar immediately.

Step 6: Set Your Greeting and Tone

Write a custom greeting or use the generated one. Adjust the tone — professional, friendly, formal, casual. A law firm sounds different from a hair salon. The AI adapts.

Step 7: Deploy

Two deployment options: web chat and voice. You can run one or both.

Web Chat Receptionist

The web chat widget goes on your website. It handles the same role as a phone receptionist but via text. Visitors ask questions, book appointments, and get routed — all without picking up the phone.

To add the chat widget to your site, paste this embed code before the closing </body> tag:

<script
  src="https://sphinxagent.ai/embed.js"
  data-agent-id="YOUR_AGENT_ID"
  data-position="bottom-right"
  data-theme="auto"
  async>
</script>

Replace YOUR_AGENT_ID with the ID from your dashboard. The widget auto-detects light and dark mode, matches your site's color scheme, and loads asynchronously so it does not slow down your page.

Available on all plans, including free.

Voice Channel Setup: AI Phone Receptionist

This is where it gets interesting. On the Pro plan ($49/month), you can provision a dedicated phone number that your AI receptionist answers directly.

How to Set Up Voice

  1. Upgrade to Pro. Go to Settings → Billing → select the Pro plan.
  2. Provision a number. Go to your agent's settings → Voice Channel → Get Phone Number. Choose a local or toll-free number. You pick the area code.
  3. Configure the voice greeting. Write what the AI says when it picks up. Example: "Thank you for calling Riverside Dental. I am the office assistant. I can help you schedule an appointment, answer questions about our services, or connect you with a team member. How can I help?"
  4. Set call routing rules. Define when calls should transfer to a live person. Examples: "Transfer to Dr. Kim if the caller says it is a dental emergency." "Transfer to front desk during business hours if the caller asks for a human."
  5. Configure after-hours behavior. After business hours, the AI can take messages, schedule next-day appointments, or provide emergency contact information.
  6. Test it. Call the number yourself. Walk through a few scenarios: scheduling, general questions, after-hours, transfer requests. Adjust the knowledge base if the AI misses anything.

You can forward your existing business phone number to the AI number, or use the AI number as your primary line. Most businesses start by forwarding overflow calls — when no one picks up after 3 rings, it routes to the AI.

Industries That Benefit Most

Medical and Dental Offices

The average medical office misses 30% of inbound calls. Patients call to schedule, reschedule, ask about insurance, request prescription refills, and get directions. An AI receptionist handles all of it. It knows which provider handles which specialties, understands insurance terminology, and can triage urgency. When a patient says "I think I am having a reaction to my medication," it escalates immediately.

Law Firms

Potential clients call law firms at all hours. A personal injury case does not wait for business hours. The AI qualifies the lead — what type of case, when it happened, jurisdiction — and routes it to the right attorney. It can schedule consultations and explain your fee structure. For firms doing intake, this alone pays for the service many times over.

Real Estate

Buyers and sellers call when they see a listing or a sign. That window of interest is short. An AI receptionist captures the lead, answers questions about the property, and schedules a showing — even on a Sunday evening. Agents who respond within 5 minutes are 21 times more likely to qualify the lead than those who wait 30 minutes.

Salons and Spas

Booking is the entire business. When stylists are with clients, nobody is at the front desk. The AI handles booking, knows each stylist's schedule and specialties, answers questions about services and pricing, and manages the waitlist. No more lost appointments because the phone rang while everyone had their hands full.

Home Services and Contractors

Plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs, and general contractors are on job sites all day. They cannot answer the phone. The AI captures the lead, asks what the issue is, gets the address, checks the service area, and books an estimate. By the time the contractor is back in the truck, there is a qualified lead waiting in their inbox with all the details.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

Sphinx Agent starts free for web chat with 100 conversations per month. The Starter plan is $29/month with scheduling. The Pro plan is $49/month with voice and a dedicated phone number. Enterprise is custom. Compared to a human receptionist at $1,500+/month or an answering service at $0.75-$1.50 per call, the economics heavily favor AI for handling routine inquiries.

Can an AI receptionist schedule appointments?

Yes. It connects to your Google Calendar, Outlook, or Calendly, reads your real-time availability, and books appointments directly. It confirms the service type, duration, and provider. The appointment shows up on your calendar immediately and the client gets a confirmation message. Available on Starter plan and above.

Does an AI receptionist work after hours?

Yes — and this is where it delivers the most value. It runs 24/7. After-hours callers and website visitors get immediate responses instead of voicemail. The AI answers questions, schedules next-day appointments, captures messages with urgency levels, and escalates emergencies to your on-call staff. Most service businesses find that 30-40% of their inquiries come outside normal hours. That is revenue you are currently leaving on the table.

Will callers know they are talking to an AI?

The AI identifies itself honestly. It does not pretend to be human. But the conversations are natural, responsive, and genuinely helpful — which is more than most callers can say about hold music and phone trees. Callers care about getting their question answered and their appointment booked. The AI does that faster than a human receptionist in most cases.

Can I still transfer calls to a live person?

Yes. You set the rules. "Always transfer if the caller asks for a specific person by name." "Transfer during business hours if the caller requests a human." "Transfer immediately for emergencies." The AI handles what it can and hands off what it cannot.
