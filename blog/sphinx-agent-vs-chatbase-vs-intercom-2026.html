Sphinx Agent vs Chatbase vs Intercom vs Tidio: Which AI Agent Platform Wins in 2026?

Head-to-head comparison of four AI chatbot platforms. Features, pricing (checked 2026-10-30), channels, and what each one does vs. what it claims.

Terrell K. Flautt

Founder, SnapIT Software

There are dozens of AI chatbot platforms. Most of them are the same widget with a different logo slapped on top.

I have used all four of the platforms in this article. I built one of them. So yes, I am biased. But I will tell you exactly where each one wins and where it falls short, because you are here to make a decision, not to read a press release.

Here is how the four platforms that actually matter stack up — and why we built Sphinx Agent to fill the gaps the others leave open.

The Quick Verdict

If you do not want to read 3,000 words, here is the answer:

  • Sphinx Agent: Best for small businesses that want a free plan, a low flat price, and an AI agent for their website chat and share link (with browser voice and lead capture) without a sales call.
  • Chatbase: Best when the bot must train on many website pages and documents.
  • Intercom: Best for enterprise teams with big budgets that want a full helpdesk suite with ticketing, macros, and Salesforce integrations.
  • Tidio: Best for small e-commerce stores running Shopify that need live chat + basic AI + Instagram DMs.

Still here? Good. Let us get into the details.

Where Sphinx Agent Wins

A Free Plan That Renews, and a Low Flat Price

Sphinx Agent is free for 100 AI messages every month, with no trial clock. Starter is $19/month ($190/year) for 3 agents and 1,500 messages; Pro is $49/month ($490/year) for 10 agents and 4,000 messages. No per-seat pricing. No per-resolution surcharges. When the month's allowance is used, the agent pauses until the 1st instead of billing you extra.

Compare that with Chatbase Hobby at $40/month for 700 credits, or Tidio, where the Lyro AI agent is a separate add-on from $32.50/month (both checked 2026-10-30).

10 Agent Types, Not Just "Support Bot"

Sphinx Agent ships 10 pre-configured agent types: Sales, Support, Customer Service, Interview, Booking, Receptionist, Lead Capture, Onboarding, FAQ, and Real Estate. Each one comes with different default behaviors and goals.

A Booking agent collects the visitor's preferred time and contact details so your team can confirm the appointment (it does not write to your calendar). A Lead Capture agent qualifies prospects and collects contact info. A Receptionist agent answers common questions in your website chat and takes messages for the team.

Pick the Right Model for the Job

Sphinx Agent lets you choose per agent: Claude Haiku, Gemini Flash or GPT-4o mini on every plan, and Claude Sonnet or GPT-4o on Pro for harder conversations. A premium-model reply counts as 3 messages, so you decide where the extra accuracy is worth it.

Leads Land in Your Inbox

When a visitor shares an email or phone number, Sphinx Agent saves it as a lead in your dashboard and emails it to you. No integration to set up first.

Browser Voice on Every Plan

Visitors can talk to your agent by voice in the browser, on every plan including Free. Dedicated AI phone lines are coming soon; they are not sold yet, so if you need a phone number answered today, Sphinx Agent is not that product yet.

Where Chatbase Wins

Chatbase trains on your content. If your answers live in many web pages or documents, Chatbase can learn from them directly, while Sphinx Agent needs you to paste the key facts. Upload your content, customize the widget, embed the script tag.

The widget customization is slightly more polished. You get more granular control over colors, positioning, and initial messages without touching code.

If you want a "chat with our docs" button over a large document library and the price fits, Chatbase is a good fit. It does that well.

Where Intercom Wins

Intercom is not really competing in the same category. It is a full helpdesk suite that happens to have an AI agent (Fin) bolted on.

If you have a 10+ person support team, you need Intercom's stuff: ticketing system, shared team inbox, macros, workflow automations, SLA tracking. The integrations ecosystem is mature — Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Slack, Jira. It all works.

Fin AI has a strong track record, and Intercom publishes resolution-rate figures for it across its customer base (see intercom.com for the current number). Credit where it is due.

But Intercom is built for enterprise teams with enterprise budgets. If you are a startup, a solo founder, or a small team, Intercom's pricing will eat your lunch before you get any value from the platform.

Where Tidio Wins

Tidio owns the small e-commerce niche. If you run a Shopify store, Tidio has a native app that installs in two clicks.

Instagram and Facebook Messenger integration is baked in. If your customers are DMing you on Instagram asking about order status, Tidio handles that. Sphinx Agent does not.

Tidio also does live chat + AI hybrid well. A human agent can watch the AI conversation in real time and jump in when needed. The handoff is smooth.

Sphinx Agent has no live-chat inbox for your team at all: it answers on its own and emails you leads. If you want humans replying live alongside the AI, Tidio is the better tool.

What Each Platform Actually Is

Strip away the marketing and here is what you are buying:

  • Sphinx Agent is an AI website agent builder for small businesses. Ten agent templates, several AI models, a website widget, share link and browser voice, with leads emailed to you. Phone lines are coming soon.
  • Chatbase is a chatbot builder that trains on your content. It is good at that purpose.
  • Intercom is a helpdesk with an AI add-on. The AI (Fin) is strong, but it lives inside a much larger (and more expensive) customer support platform.
  • Tidio is a live chat tool with AI sprinkled in. The AI (Lyro) handles what it can; humans handle the rest. Best suited for e-commerce.

These are different products solving different problems. Pick the one that matches what you actually need, not the one with the best landing page.

The Bottom Line

If you are a small business that wants an AI agent on its website for free or a low flat price, with leads emailed to you: Sphinx Agent.

If your bot must learn from lots of web pages and documents: Chatbase.

If you have a large support team and need a full helpdesk with ticketing and CRM integrations: Intercom.

If you run a Shopify store and want Instagram DM automation with live chat fallback: Tidio.

No sales calls. No per-seat pricing that punishes you for hiring.
