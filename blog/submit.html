We publish original articles and research papers on AI strategy, alignment, safety, governance, and applied AI engineering. Submissions are reviewed by our editorial team and published if they meet our standards.

Submission Guidelines

  • 1.Original content only. Your article must not be published elsewhere. We do not accept duplicate or syndicated content.
  • 2.Topics we cover: AI alignment, AI safety, autonomous agents, AI governance, applied AI/ML engineering, LLM architecture, AI policy, and the intersection of technology and society.
  • 3.Quality standards: Well-researched, cite sources where applicable, clear writing, and a strong thesis. Minimum 1,000 words.
  • 4.Format: Submit as plain text, Markdown, or HTML. Include a suggested title and 1-2 sentence summary.
  • 5.Review timeline: We aim to respond within 7 business days. Not all submissions will be published.
