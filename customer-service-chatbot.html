Features

Everything You Need for Automated Customer Service

Sphinx Agent is purpose-built for customer service automation. Train it on your content, customize its behavior, and let it handle the rest.

Custom Q&A Overrides

Define exact answers for critical questions like pricing, policies, and procedures. Your chatbot delivers pixel-perfect responses for the topics that matter most to your business.

Smart Escalation Paths

Automatically detect when a conversation needs a human touch. Route complex or sensitive issues to the right team member via email, Slack, or your existing helpdesk system.

Multi-Language Support

Serve customers in their preferred language. Sphinx Agent detects the visitor's language automatically and responds fluently, expanding your reach without translation costs.

Full Conversation History

Review every conversation your chatbot has handled. Search, filter, and export transcripts to identify trends, improve answers, and train your team on real customer questions.
