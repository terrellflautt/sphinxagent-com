Cookie Policy

Last Updated: March 17, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit.

2. Our Approach

Sphinx Agent does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies. We believe in minimal data collection and respect your privacy.

3. What We Use Instead

We use localStorage (browser-based storage) for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: Your login session token is stored in localStorage so you stay signed in between visits.
  • Theme Preference: Your light/dark mode choice is saved locally.
  • Language Preference: Your English/Spanish language selection is stored locally.
  • Cookie Consent: Whether you've acknowledged this notice is saved locally.

4. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services may set their own cookies when you interact with them:

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in with Google, Google may set authentication-related cookies. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe: When you make a payment, Stripe may set fraud-prevention cookies. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

5. Managing Your Data

You can clear all locally stored data at any time by:

  • Clearing your browser's localStorage (Settings > Privacy > Clear Browsing Data)
  • Using your browser's Developer Tools to manually remove specific keys
  • Logging out, which clears your authentication data
